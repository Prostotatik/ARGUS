### 2. [Medium] Classifier generalisation + real SPAM-TLD bug - FIXED (root cause) + honestly-reported generalisation ceiling.

Root-cause fix: the TLD nudge is now computed **after** all content/attachment signals, and is suppressed or shrunk by countervailing evidence instead of always firing at full weight:

- no other signal at all -> full +3 nudge (unchanged behaviour for a genuinely bare, unattributable message).
